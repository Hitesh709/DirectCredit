New customer & loan journey

Create the customer profile first, complete the application checklist, attach documents and submit the loan request.

1. Customer identity & contact

Enter the primary applicant's personal details and reliable contact information.

2. Residential & communication address

Capture current and permanent address information for verification and field visits.

3. Business & income profile

These details support business verification and repayment-capacity assessment.

4. Banking details

Provide the account used for business income and loan disbursement. Never enter a PIN, password or OTP.

Bank details are sensitive. They should only be submitted over the configured secure API and shown to authorized staff.

5. KYC & supporting documents

Attach clear, readable documents. Uploads are optional at this stage but can be completed before final review.

Supported: PDF, JPG, PNG up to 2 MB per file. Avoid uploading unmasked Aadhaar or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Loan requirement

Record the customer's requested product, amount, purpose and preferred repayment term.

7. Review before submission

Review the key information below. Customer profile and loan application are submitted through the existing DirectCredit API.

Submitting creates a customer record first and then creates the loan application. If the second step fails, the message will show the customer ID so you can continue without creating a duplicate.
